About BookClub

BookClub is a digital community dedicated to making books discoverable, readable, and shareable for everyone.

We bring together readers, librarians, and volunteers to build a modern, open library experience. Our goal is to create one page for every published book and make it easy for readers around the world to find and enjoy new stories.

Our Vision

A world where every published work is accessible to readers everywhere, regardless of location or background.

Our Mission

To build an open platform for books, powered by community contributions and a simple, beautiful reading experience.

Get Involved

Help improve book records, add new titles, or spread the word so more readers can discover BookClub.

Why BookClub matters

BookClub is built on the belief that knowledge should be open and easy to access. We support readers by making books searchable, borrowable, and shareable. Our community helps ensure every title gets the accurate metadata and discoverability it deserves.

  • Explore a growing catalog of books.
  • Contribute by improving book records.
  • Help others find the stories they love.
Ways to support

Join the community, suggest new books, or volunteer to help curate the collection. Every contribution makes BookClub stronger.
